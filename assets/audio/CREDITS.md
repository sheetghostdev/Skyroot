# Skyroot audio credits

All audio in this folder is CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication).
Curated files come from Kenney's CC0 packs (www.kenney.nl; see LICENSE-Kenney.txt) and were trimmed, mixed to mono, loudness-normalised and re-encoded (Ogg Vorbis q6) by `tools/audio/curate.py`; extra processing is noted per file. Everything else was generated from scratch by `tools/audio/synth.py` (numpy procedural synthesis) and is likewise released under CC0.

Regenerate with:

    python3 tools/audio/synth.py
    python3 tools/audio/curate.py /path/to/kenney   # needs ffmpeg
